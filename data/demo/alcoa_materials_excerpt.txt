Safety across mining and refining operations is managed under the Critical Risk Management program. In 2024, Alcoa recorded zero work-related fatalities, and the Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) declined to 1.12 per 200,000 hours worked. Active bauxite mine rehabilitation covered 1,420 hectares.

Alcoa produces low-carbon aluminum under the EcoLum brand, generating less than 4.0 metric tons of CO2e per ton of aluminum produced. Scope 1 and Scope 2 direct greenhouse gas emissions were reduced by 31% against the 2015 baseline year.

Workforce diversity initiatives increased female representation in professional and leadership roles to 28.5%. In the supply chain, 100% of major suppliers were evaluated against the Sustainable Procurement Standard and Supplier Code of Conduct.

The Ethics and Compliance program enforces zero-tolerance policies for bribery and corruption. During the reporting year, 99.4% of eligible salaried personnel completed comprehensive anti-corruption and compliance training modules.
